# Commute cost optimiser design

The demo should answer one question quickly: what is the cheapest way to pay for a regular round trip?

- Treat the planner as a return ticket, with home, work and home again stated on the ticket edge.
- Keep both travel times visible. The return leg materially changes peak and off-peak pricing.
- Show the recommendation first, then only the two closest alternatives.
- Use the muted green of old ticket stock as the sole accent against a quiet charcoal ground.
- Preserve real station lookup, fare tables, caps and passes. Do not imply live fare data.
- Keep advanced assumptions in the underlying calculator without making the first decision harder to read.
